# Marketing4Startups brand strategy

## Brand idea

**Find your next reliable source of customers.** Marketing4Startups helps founders make clear, low-cost growth decisions before they spend heavily.

## Positioning

For startup founders and small growth teams who need customers but cannot afford guesswork, Marketing4Startups is a practical growth workspace that turns customer evidence into small channel tests, then checks the cost before recommending more spend.

Unlike a collection of marketing tips, it connects customer conversations, clear product messaging, channel experiments, and spending limits in one repeatable process.

## Promise and tagline

- **Primary tagline:** Find customers with evidence, not guesswork.
- **Supporting line:** Try small. Learn quickly. Grow what works.
- **Product promise:** Every growth idea has a clear question, a spending limit, a result to watch, and a next step.

## Who it serves

1. Founders finding their first repeatable source of customers.
2. Small marketing teams that need to show what each euro produces.
3. Advisors helping startups focus on a clear customer and a practical plan.

## Brand personality and voice

**The experienced operator:** calm, direct, curious, and careful with other people's money.

- Use everyday words before framework names or acronyms.
- Explain a metric in the same sentence where it first appears.
- Separate observed results from estimates and ideas.
- Give one useful next step; avoid hype, guarantees, and pressure.

## Message order

1. **Outcome:** Find a dependable way to reach customers.
2. **Method:** Learn what customers already do, then test a few small ideas.
3. **Proof:** Show the cost, customer response, and result for each test.
4. **Guardrail:** Spend more only when customer value can support the cost.

## Logo concept

The mark combines a three-ring target with an arrow that exits toward the upper right. The rings stand for focus and testing; the arrow shows progress earned through evidence. The dark tile gives the symbol a confident, product-ready silhouette at small sizes. A restrained lime accent marks the path forward.

Use the square mark for the app icon and compact placements. Use the horizontal lockup for the site header, documents, and presentations. Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of its center dot. Do not add gradients, shadows, extra rings, or decorative growth charts.

## Visual system

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Focus cobalt | `#2441C4` | Primary actions, links, active navigation, and the center of the target |
| Ink | `#14211F` | Headlines, body text, and the logo tile |
| Mist | `#EDF0EE` | Main workspace background |
| Porcelain | `#F8FAF9` | Cards and reading surfaces |
| Ring periwinkle | `#93A6EC` | Outer target ring and secondary data |
| Ring frost | `#DCE3FA` | Inner ring and quiet highlights |
| Traction green | `#1B7A43` | Passing economics and confirmed results |
| Signal lime | `#B9F274` | A small logo accent; use sparingly |
| Caution amber | `#8F5B00` | Needs review or uncertain results |
| Stop red | `#B3261E` | Failed guardrails or urgent issues |

Use the device system sans-serif for headings and product copy, with a system monospace for figures, dates, and short labels. This keeps the app self-contained and avoids sending visitor requests to a font provider. Keep body text spacious and readable; reserve monospace for short, scan-friendly details.

## Product design rules

- Keep cobalt for actions and focus; use green, amber, and red only for result states.
- Show the spending limits beside the work, not hidden in settings.
- Use the target rings to explain the channel search: ideas, small tests, proven focus.
- On small screens, preserve access to every section and let wide tables scroll instead of shrinking their text.
- Keep the app name as **Marketing4Startups**. Use **M4S** only after the full name has appeared.
